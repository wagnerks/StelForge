Ground materials from FreeStylized (https://freestylized.com), free for commercial and non-commercial use;
redistributing the textures on their own is not allowed.

grass    - Ground 05 (grass)        https://freestylized.com/material/ground_05/
moss     - Ground 02 (bare earth, road)   https://freestylized.com/material/ground_02/
mud      - Ground 08 (bank, lake bed)     https://freestylized.com/material/ground_08/
sand     - Ground 08 (bank)       https://freestylized.com/material/ground_08/
rocks    - Ground with Rocks 03   https://freestylized.com/material/ground_with_rocks_03/

litter   - Ground with Roots 01   https://freestylized.com/material/ground_with_roots_01/
track    - Ground 03 (between the road ruts)   https://freestylized.com/material/ground_03/

Packed per material: <name>_albedo_height.png (rgb base colour, a height) and
<name>_normal_rough_ao.png (rg OpenGL normal xy, b roughness, a ambient occlusion).
All but rocks are packed by scripts/pack_freestylized_textures.py.
